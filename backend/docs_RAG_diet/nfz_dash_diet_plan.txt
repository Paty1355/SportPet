Day 1 (1799 kcal)

Breakfast (454 kcal) | protein 27 g | fat 23 g | carbs 38 g | 15 min
Oatmeal pancakes with strawberries
- 2% fat milk ⅓ cup, 100 ml
- oat flour 3 tbsp, 30 g
- chicken eggs 1 piece, 50 g
- canola oil 1 tbsp, 10 ml
- lean curd/cottage cheese 1 ⅓ slice, 40 g
- 0% fat plain yogurt 2 ½ tbsp, 50 g
- fresh or frozen strawberries ½ cup, 70 g
- flaxseed 1 tsp, 5 g

Prepare the pancake batter - mix flour, egg, and milk (you can add water if the batter is too thick). Fry the pancakes in oil. Mash the strawberries into a mousse. Add flaxseed to it and mix. Mix the cheese with the yogurt into a smooth paste. Fill the pancakes with the prepared stuffing. Pour the strawberry mousse over the pancakes on a plate.
Dish contains: lactose, gluten

Morning Snack / Second Breakfast (220 kcal) | protein 4 g | fat 11 g | carbs 27 g | 15 min
Cherry tomato, arugula, and walnut salad with toast
- walnuts 2 pieces, 8 g
- rye bread 1 slice, 35 g
- mustard ½ tsp, 5 g
- wine vinegar 1 tsp, 5 ml
- canola oil 1 tsp, 5 ml
- cherry tomatoes 6 pieces, 120 g
- black pepper 1 pinch, 1 g
- arugula 1 handful, 25 g
- lettuce 2 leaves, 10 g

Wash all vegetables thoroughly. Combine canola oil, vinegar, mustard, and pepper, set aside. Toast the bread in a toaster or pan. Tear the lettuce into pieces, cut the tomatoes in half. Chop the walnuts. Arrange lettuce, arugula, and tomatoes on a plate, sprinkle with nuts, and drizzle with the previously prepared dressing. Eat with the toast.
Dish contains: gluten

Lunch (550 kcal) | protein 37 g | fat 16 g | carbs 64 g | 30 min
Chicken skewers
- olive oil 1 tbsp, 10 ml
- unroasted buckwheat groats, dry 6 tbsp, 60 g
- zucchini ⅙ piece, 100 g
- skinless chicken breast fillet ½ piece, 120 g
- red bell pepper 1 piece, 230 g
- onion 1 piece, 100 g

Cook the groats. Wash the vegetables and clean the meat. Cut them into cubes. Thread them alternately onto skewer sticks. Drizzle with olive oil. Season with your favorite spices. Bake the skewers at 180 degrees Celsius for about 25 minutes. Serve with the groats.
Dish contains: meat

Day 2 (1798 kcal)

Breakfast (447 kcal) | protein 12 g | fat 13 g | carbs 74 g | 5 min
Stracciatella oatmeal
- flaxseed 1 tsp, 5 g
- banana 1 piece, 120 g
- blueberries 1 handful, 50 g
- dark chocolate 3 squares, 15 g

Peel the banana and cut it into slices. Chop the chocolate. Pour boiling water over the oats and leave for a few minutes. Then add plain yogurt, banana slices, and chopped chocolate. Mix and sprinkle with flaxseed, add blueberries.

Morning Snack (220 kcal) | protein 13 g | fat 7 g | carbs 29 g | 35 min
Millet muffin with vegetables
- chicken eggs 1 piece, 50 g
- zucchini ⅙ piece, 80 g
- garlic ½ clove, 3 g
- dry millet 2 ½ tbsp, 25 g
- red bell pepper ½ piece, 120 g
- dried basil 1 pinch, 1 g
- sweet paprika powder 1 pinch, 1 g
- chives 1 tbsp, 5 g

Cook the millet according to the package instructions. Grate the zucchini and pepper on the large holes of a grater. Finely chop the chives, press the garlic through a garlic press, and beat the eggs. Combine all ingredients. Transfer the prepared mixture to silicone molds (like for muffins/cupcakes) and bake for about 25 minutes in an oven preheated to 190 degrees Celsius.

Chia pudding with strawberries

Lunch (594 kcal) | protein 36 g | fat 22 g | carbs 69 g | 40 min
Stuffed zucchini in tomato sauce
- minced turkey breast ½ cup, 90 g
- chili pepper powder 1 pinch, 1 g
- dried thyme 1 pinch, 1 g
- tomato passata ¼ cup, 60 g
- garlic 1 clove, 5 g
- onion ½ piece, 50 g
- dried basil 1 pinch, 1 g
- olive oil 1 tsp, 5 ml
- parsley, fresh 1 tsp, 6 g
- mozzarella cheese 1 slice, 20 g
- brown rice, dry ¼ cup, 50 g
- green beans, fresh or frozen ⅔ cup, 80 g
- flaxseed oil 1 tbsp, 10 ml

Mix the meat with the spices and parsley. Cut the zucchini in half lengthwise and carefully remove the center so as not to damage the skin. Add the center to the meat with spices and mix again. Put the prepared stuffing into the hollowed zucchini. Bake the stuffed zucchini in the oven for about 35 minutes at 180 degrees. Near the end of baking, sprinkle it with grated mozzarella. In the meantime, sauté the onion and garlic in olive oil. Add the tomato passata, simmer for a while (add basil at the end). Cook the rice according to the package instructions. Boil the green beans and drizzle with flaxseed oil. Pour the tomato sauce over the baked zucchini. Eat it with rice and green beans.
Dish contains: lactose, meat

Afternoon Snack (153 kcal) | protein 5.9 g | fat 2.6 g | carbs 27.5 g | 20 min
Strawberry-millet pudding
- millet 2 ½ tbsp, 25 g
- 2% fat milk ⅓ cup, 80 ml
- frozen or fresh strawberries ½ cup, 75 g

Pour the millet into a saucepan, rinse under warm, running water, changing it several times. Drain, pour in the milk, and bring to a boil covered. Cook for 15 minutes over low heat without opening the pot. Blend the cooked millet into a smooth mousse, adding the washed and hulled strawberries. If the pudding is too thick, you can add more fruit.
Dish contains: lactose

Lunch (512 kcal) | protein 35.5 g | fat 12.9 g | carbs 77.7 g | 50 min
Chicken stewed in carrots and tomatoes
- chicken breast fillet 1 piece, 120 g
- onion ½ piece, 50 g
- carrot 1 piece, 45 g
- canola oil 1 tbsp, 10 ml
- tomato 2 pieces, 300 g
- potatoes 3 pieces, 280 g

Steam or boil the potatoes in water. Chop the vegetables - dice the onion and tomato, cut the carrot into strips. Sauté the onion briefly in oil, then add the carrot (you can add a little water if needed) and simmer covered for about 15 minutes. When the carrot becomes soft, add the tomato and place the previously seasoned chicken on top of the vegetables and stew for another 20 minutes.
Dish contains: meat

Day 4 (1799 kcal)

Breakfast (470 kcal) | protein 39.1 g | fat 22.3 g | carbs 25.9 g | 20 min
Turkey and vegetable omelet with toast
- chicken eggs 2 pieces, 100 g
- onion ½ piece, 50 g
- turkey breast fillet ½ piece, 100 g
- flaxseed 1 tbsp, 10 g
- canola oil 1/2 tbsp, 5 ml
- red bell pepper 1/5 piece, 50 g
- arugula 1 handful, 25 g
- pepper to taste, 1 g
- salt to taste, 1 g
- herbes de provence to taste, 1 g
- graham bread 1 slice, 30 g
- avocado ¼ piece, 35 g
- garlic ⅓ clove, 3 g
- sweet paprika powder to taste, 2 g

Beat the eggs in a bowl, aerating them well. Add herbes de provence, ground flaxseed, salt, and pepper. Toss the diced turkey, onion, and red pepper onto a greased pan. Sauté briefly, then pour the eggs over. Lower the heat and cover the pan with a lid. The omelet can be cooked until completely set or fried on both sides (slide onto a plate, then slide back into the pan, other side down). Arrange arugula on a plate, then the omelet (some arugula can also be placed inside). Toast the bread (in a toaster or grill). Mix the soft avocado with garlic pressed through a press and season with your favorite spices. Spread the resulting paste on the warm toast.
Dish contains: gluten, meat
