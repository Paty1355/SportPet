Types of diets, their characteristics, recommended and contraindicated foodstuffs used in individual types of diets, and the nutritional and energy value of hospital diets used for adults

====================================================================
1. BASIC DIET (DIETA PODSTAWOWA)
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• For hospitalized individuals not requiring special dietary modifications.
• Basic nutrition should meet all conditions of proper nutrition for healthy people and forms the basis for planning therapeutic diets.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Energy and nutritional value is adjusted to the patient's body weight and physiological state, based on current nutritional standards for the Polish population.
• The diet should be properly balanced and varied regarding food groups.
• Meals should be served 3-5 times a day. Snacking between meals should be eliminated.
• Addition of vegetables or fruits to every meal (minimum 400 g daily - potatoes and sweet potatoes are not included), with a predominance of vegetables. At least some should be served raw.
• At least 1 meal a day should contain whole-grain cereal products.
• Milk and dairy products should be served in at least 2 meals a day.
• Every day, at least 1 portion from the meat, eggs, or legumes group must be served. Legumes should be included at least 3 times in a 10-day menu.
• Fish should be included at least 3 times in a 10-day menu.
• Animal fats should be limited in favor of plant fats.
• Added sugars and salt should be minimized.

ENERGY VALUE: 2000-2400 kcal/day
NUTRITIONAL VALUE:
• Protein: 10-20% of Energy (25-50 g/1000 kcal)
• Total Fat: 25-30% of Energy (28-33 g/1000 kcal)
• Saturated Fat: <10% of Energy
• Total Carbohydrates: 45-65% of Energy (113-163 g/1000 kcal)
• Sugars: <10% of Energy
• Fiber: 15 g/1000 kcal
• Sodium: ≤2000 mg/day

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Cereal products: Whole-grain flours, whole-wheat bread, rye bread, graham bread, groats (buckwheat, barley, spelt, bulgur, millet), natural flakes, brown rice, whole-grain pasta.
• Vegetables/Fruits: All fresh and frozen vegetables and fruits (boiled, stewed, baked, fermented). 
• Meat/Fish/Eggs: Lean meat (poultry without skin, lean beef/pork), all sea and freshwater fish, eggs boiled, poached, or scrambled with minimal fat.
• Dairy: Milk (1.5-2% fat), lean/semi-fat curd cheese, natural fermented milk beverages without added sugar.
• Fats: Vegetable oils (rapeseed, olive oil), soft margarines, butter (limited).
• Beverages: Still water, weak tea, herbal infusions, chicory coffee, vegetable juices.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Bread with added sugar/syrups, deep-fried dough products (donuts), sweetened breakfast cereals.
• Vegetables/Fruits prepared with large amounts of fat; fruits in heavy syrups, candied fruits.
• Fatty meats (duck, goose, fatty pork/beef), fatty cold cuts (bacon, salami), deep-fried meats/fish, raw meat/fish.
• Eggs prepared with a lot of fat/lard/bacon.
• Fatty dairy, sweetened yogurts, condensed milk.
• Animal fats (lard, tallow), hard margarines, coconut/palm oil.
• Highly sweetened beverages, energy drinks, alcohol.

2. EASILY DIGESTIBLE DIET (DIETA ŁATWOSTRAWNA)
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• For individuals after surgical procedures, during convalescence, with inflammatory diseases of the stomach and intestines, gastroesophageal reflux, chronic hepatitis, chronic pancreatitis, feverish infections, elderly patients.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• A modification of the basic diet involving the appropriate choice of culinary techniques (e.g., boiling in water/steam, pureeing, blending) to improve digestibility.
• Excludes bloating and heavy/indigestible foods.
• Reduced amount of insoluble dietary fiber. 
• Meals served 3-5 times a day regularly.

ENERGY VALUE: 2000-2400 kcal/day
NUTRITIONAL VALUE: Similar to the Basic Diet, but tailored for easier digestion.

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Cereal products: Medium and low-extraction flours (wheat, potato, corn, rice), white wheat/rye bread, graham bread, fine groats (semolina, corn, millet), fine pasta, white rice.
• Vegetables/Fruits: Fresh tender vegetables finely grated, boiled/stewed/baked without fat (carrots, beets, zucchini, pumpkin, spinach, skinless tomatoes). Ripe fruits without skin/seeds, fruit purees/mousses.
• Meat/Fish/Eggs: Lean meat and cold cuts, all lean fish (steamed, baked, boiled). Eggs soft-boiled or steamed.
• Dairy: Lean/semi-fat milk and curd, low-fat natural fermented beverages.
• Fats: Butter (limited), soft margarine, vegetable oils (rapeseed, olive oil).

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Whole-grain flours, coarse groats (buckwheat, pearl barley), brown rice, bran.
• Cruciferous vegetables (cabbage, Brussels sprouts), raw onion/garlic/leek, dry legumes (beans, peas).
• Unripe fruits, heavy fruits (cherries, pears, plums), dried fruits.
• Fatty meats/fish, raw meats/fish, fried meats, smoked fish, canned fish in oil.
• Hard-boiled eggs, fried eggs.
• Lard, tallow, hard margarines, coconut/palm fat.

3. EASILY DIGESTIBLE DIET WITH FAT RESTRICTION
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Acute and chronic hepatitis, liver cirrhosis, acute/chronic pancreatitis, chronic inflammation of the gallbladder and biliary tract, gallstones, exacerbation of ulcerative colitis or Crohn's disease.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Modification of the easily digestible diet focusing on reducing products rich in animal fat and cholesterol.
• Limit saturated fats (red meat, offal, coconut/palm oil, sweets, fast food).
• Exclude products rich in cholesterol (fatty meats, egg yolks, fatty dairy).
• With low fat intake, ensure adequate supply of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K).

ENERGY VALUE: 2000-2400 kcal/day
NUTRITIONAL VALUE:
• Total Fat: 15-25% of Energy (17-28 g/1000 kcal)
• Saturated Fat: <7% of Energy (<7.8 g/1000 kcal)

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Leanest meats (skinless poultry, lean veal/beef), lean cold cuts.
• Lean sea and freshwater fish (steamed/baked).
• Egg whites (unlimited), whole eggs (with restricted yolks).
• Skimmed or very low-fat dairy products.
• Limited amounts of vegetable oils (rapeseed, olive oil) and soft margarines.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• All fatty meats, fatty fish, canned fish in oil, offal (pate, liver sausage).
• Egg yolks in excess, fried eggs.
• Full-fat dairy, heavy cream, yellow/ripened cheeses.
• Lard, tallow, butter (unless strictly limited), hard margarines.
• Sweets with large amounts of fat (cakes with creams, puff pastry, chocolate).


====================================================================
4. EASILY DIGESTIBLE DIET WITH RESTRICTION OF GASTRIC JUICE STIMULATING SUBSTANCES
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Gastric inflammatory diseases, gastroesophageal reflux, gastric/duodenal ulcers, functional gastric dyspepsia, chronic hyperacidic gastritis.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Limits foods that stimulate gastric acid secretion (e.g., strong broths, spicy seasonings, acidic products).
• Soups should be made on vegetable broths only (no meat/bone broths).
• Elimination of added sugars and strong extracts.

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Refined grain products, white rice, fine groats.
• Young, tender, cooked vegetables (zucchini, carrots, pumpkin); skinless tomatoes (limited).
• Ripe, cooked, or pureed fruits (without skin/seeds).
• Lean meat/fish prepared by boiling or steaming.
• Mild spices: bay leaf, allspice, dill, thyme, basil, oregano.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Strong meat/bone broths, mushroom broths.
• Pickled, marinated, salted, or canned vegetables/fruits.
• Raw, unripe, or highly acidic fruits (citrus, unripe apples).
• Spicy seasonings: black pepper, cayenne, chili, mustard, vinegar, soy sauce.
• Coffee (especially strong), strong tea, alcohol, carbonated drinks.

5. HIGH-RESIDUE (HIGH-FIBER) DIET
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Habitual constipation, atonic constipation, irritable bowel syndrome (constipation-predominant), prevention of diverticulosis, colon cancer, gallstones, obesity, lipid/carbohydrate metabolism disorders.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Increase dietary fiber to about 40-50 g/day, mostly insoluble fiber (whole grains, raw vegetables, specific fruits).
• Fluid intake must be increased to >2.5 liters/day to allow fiber to swell and function properly.
• Minimum of 3 portions of high-fiber vegetables daily, served mostly raw.
• Whole-grain products in at least 2 meals a day.

NUTRITIONAL VALUE:
• Fiber: >35 g/day (approx. 40-50 g/day)

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Whole-grain flours, whole-wheat, rye, graham bread, bread with added bran/seeds.
• Coarse groats (buckwheat, pearl barley), brown/red rice, bran (oat, wheat, rye).
• Raw and cooked vegetables, especially leafy greens, root vegetables.
• Fresh and dried fruits (prunes, apricots, figs - moderate amounts).
• Legumes (beans, peas, lentils, chickpeas).
• Nuts, seeds, pips (unsalted).

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Refined white flour, white bread, sweet pastries, donuts.
• Fine groats (semolina), white rice.
• Fried foods, heavily processed foods, fast food.
• Strong sweets, excessive animal fats.

6. DIET WITH RESTRICTION OF EASILY ASSIMILABLE CARBOHYDRATES
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Diabetes, insulin resistance, impaired glucose tolerance, hypertriglyceridemia.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Restriction of glucose, fructose, and sucrose. Recommended foods with a Low Glycemic Index (GI < 55).
• Regular meals (4-6 times a day) with 3-4 hour intervals.
• Carbohydrate products should not be eaten alone; protein and fat should be included in every meal to lower the overall GI.
• Pasta, groats, and rice should be cooked "al dente" (overcooked products significantly raise blood sugar levels).
• Choose less ripe fruits due to lower sugar content.

NUTRITIONAL VALUE:
• Carbohydrates: 45-65% of Energy
• Sugars (mono- and disaccharides): <10% of Energy

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Whole-grain bread, sourdough bread, bread with low GI.
• Coarse groats, brown/red rice, whole-grain pasta (cooked al dente).
• Fresh/frozen vegetables (raw preferred).
• Fruits with GI < 55, less ripe fruits.
• Lean meats, fish, eggs, legumes, nuts, seeds.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Sugar, honey, syrups, jams with added sugar, sweets, cakes.
• White bread, fine groats, instant oatmeal, rice cakes, overcooked white rice.
• Overcooked carrots, overcooked beets, baked sweet potatoes, mashed potatoes.
• Fruits with GI > 55, fruits in syrup, candied fruits, dried fruits.
• Highly sweetened beverages, fruit nectars.


====================================================================
7. DIET WITH CONTROLLED FATTY ACID CONTENT
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Cardiovascular diseases, atherosclerosis, hyperlipidemia, chronic coronary syndromes.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Reduction of saturated fatty acids and partial replacement with mono- and polyunsaturated fatty acids.
• Restriction of dietary cholesterol (<200 mg/day).
• Elimination of trans fatty acid isomers (found in confectionery fats, hard margarines, fast food).
• Emphasis on foods rich in fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) due to fat restriction.

NUTRITIONAL VALUE:
• Saturated Fat: <7% of Energy
• Monounsaturated Fat: ~20% of Energy
• Polyunsaturated Fat: 6-10% of Energy
• Cholesterol: < 200 mg/day

RECOMMENDED / CONTRAINDICATED:
• Emphasize vegetable oils (olive, rapeseed, linseed), soft margarines with sterols/stanols, fatty marine fish (salmon, mackerel, herring), nuts, and seeds.
• Exclude lard, tallow, butter, coconut oil, palm oil, fatty meats/cold cuts, offal, fried foods, and full-fat dairy.


====================================================================
8. LOW-ENERGY DIET
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Overweight and obesity (if no other contraindications).

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Reduction of caloric intake (typically 1500-1800 kcal/day) aimed at a weight loss of 0.5-1.0 kg/week.
• Increased amount of dietary fiber and adequate protein supply (0.8-1.0 g/kg body weight/day).
• Total carbohydrates should not fall below 130 g/day.
• Elimination of added sugars and limitation of fats (especially animal fats).

RECOMMENDED / CONTRAINDICATED:
• Follows principles of the High-Residue and Low-Glycemic diets, strictly controlling portion sizes and total caloric intake.
• Strongly contraindicates sweets, fast food, sweetened beverages, alcohol, and high-fat products.


====================================================================
9. HIGH-PROTEIN DIET
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Body emaciation/malnutrition, convalescence (e.g., post-surgery, stroke), severe/extensive burns, extensive wounds, cancer, compensated liver cirrhosis, nephrotic syndrome, dialysis, long-term high fever.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Protein content is increased to 1.5-2.0 g/kg of ideal body weight (or 100-130 g/day), with 1/2 to 2/3 coming from high biological value animal protein.
• In severe burns, protein may increase to 2.0-3.0 g/kg.
• May utilize oral nutritional supplements (ONS) or protein powders to fortify meals.
• 5 meals a day.

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Lean meat, poultry, fish, eggs, lean/semi-fat dairy products (curd, yogurt, kefir).
• Enrichment of meals with milk powder or egg powder if necessary.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Fatty meats, raw meats, heavily fried foods, heavy/indigestible foods.


====================================================================
10. LOW-PROTEIN DIET
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Kidney failure, liver failure, gallbladder/biliary tract diseases.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Protein intake strictly individualized based on disease stage and tolerance (e.g., 0.6-0.8 g/kg in chronic kidney disease stages 3-5 without dialysis).
• Protein must be of high biological value (animal origin - approx. 75% of total protein).
• Control of sodium, phosphorus, and potassium. In case of hyperkalemia, double-boiling of potatoes and vegetables is recommended.
• Fluid intake carefully monitored.
• Specialized low-protein or protein-free products (e.g., PKU bread, low-protein pasta) may be used.

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Low-protein/protein-free flours, starches (potato, corn, rice).
• Low-protein bread, pasta, and rusks.
• Carefully measured portions of lean meat, fish, dairy, or egg whites to meet strict protein limits.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Standard breads/pastas/flours (if strict restriction is required).
• Legumes (very high in plant protein and phosphorus).
• Salty and highly processed meats, canned foods.


====================================================================
11. ELIMINATION DIET
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Diagnosed food intolerance or allergy, medical/religious/ideological dietary exclusions.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Custom-tailored modification of the basic diet to completely exclude the offending allergen/ingredient while maintaining nutritional balance with substitutes.
• Extreme care in hygienic meal preparation to avoid cross-contamination.

COMMON ELIMINATIONS:
1. GLUTEN-FREE: Excludes wheat, rye, barley, oats (unless certified GF), triticale, spelt. Uses rice, soy, buckwheat, corn, millet, amaranth, quinoa, tapioca.
2. DAIRY-FREE / LACTOSE-FREE: Excludes cow's milk, cheese, yogurt, butter, whey, casein. Uses plant-based fortified beverages (soy, oat, almond) and vegan substitutes.
3. EGG-FREE: Excludes chicken, quail, turkey, and duck eggs, egg noodles, mayonnaise, baked goods with eggs.
4. VEGETARIAN / SEMI-VEGETARIAN: Excludes meat, poultry, and meat products (sometimes fish). Replaced with dairy, eggs, legumes (soy, lentils, chickpeas, beans, tofu, hummus), and nuts/seeds.


====================================================================
12. MUSHY / PUREED DIET (DIETA PAPKOWATA)
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Diseases of the oral cavity, esophagus (inflammation, strictures, varices, cancer), chewing and swallowing difficulties, post-surgery.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Consistency is modified to a mushy/pureed form that does not require chewing.
• Excludes foods that could irritate the esophagus/mouth (acidic, spicy, very salty).
• Meals served in regular intervals, small volumes, 3-5 times a day.
• Soups pureed/blended, meat finely ground/blended with broth, vegetables/fruits as smooth purees. Bread must be soaked in milk, tea, or soup before blending.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Any solid food requiring chewing. Nuts, seeds, whole grains, raw unblended vegetables/fruits.


====================================================================
13. LIQUID DIET (DIETA PŁYNNA)
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Severe oral/esophageal diseases, absolute intolerance to solid food (nausea, vomiting, severe diarrhea), first 24-48 hours post-surgery.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Purpose is to relieve thirst and provide hydration; nutritional value is NOT considered.
• Used for a very short time (usually up to 48 hours).
• Consists mainly of neutral liquids: boiled/still mineral water, weak tea, chamomile infusion, defatted broth, very diluted gruel water, clear fruit juices.
• Administered in small portions (30-60 ml) every 1 hour initially.


====================================================================
14. FORTIFIED LIQUID DIET (DIETA PŁYNNA WZMOCNIONA)
====================================================================
APPLICATION:
• Acute gastrointestinal catarrh, post-GI bleeding, post-surgery convalescence, severe swallowing/chewing difficulties.

DIETARY RECOMMENDATIONS:
• Unlike the clear liquid diet, this diet provides sufficient energy and nutrients to meet the body's full requirements.
• All solid foods must be thoroughly cooked, blended, and diluted to a completely liquid consistency.
• Served at room temperature (20-25°C) or body temperature (~37°C) so as not to irritate the GI tract.
• Fibrous foods are strictly limited.

RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS:
• Finely ground/blended lean meats, fish, soft-boiled eggs blended into soups.
• Fine groats/rice overcooked and pureed into gruels.
• Cooked, blended vegetables and fruits served as liquid soups/broths/juices.

CONTRAINDICATED PRODUCTS:
• Any solid chunks, raw vegetables/fruits, whole grains, seeds, nuts, fatty/fried foods, spicy seasonings.
